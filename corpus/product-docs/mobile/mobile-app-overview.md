# Mobile App Overview

The Helix mobile app gives you on-the-go visibility into your workflows.

## Platforms

- iOS 15+
- Android 9+

Download from the App Store or Google Play.

## What you can do

The mobile app is intentionally focused. You can:

- View runs
- Approve manual steps
- Receive notifications

## What you cannot do

By design, the mobile app does not include:

- Cannot edit workflows
- Cannot manage integrations
- Cannot manage billing

For these, use the web app.

## Why this design

We've found that workflow editing on a small screen leads to errors. Approval, monitoring, and visibility are well-suited to mobile; design and configuration are not.

## Authentication

The mobile app supports the same auth methods as the web app: email/password, SSO, and 2FA.

## Biometric login

Once signed in, enable Face ID (iOS) or fingerprint (Android) for quick re-authentication. Sessions last 30 days before requiring full credentials.
