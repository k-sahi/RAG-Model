# In-App Notifications

The bell icon in the top right shows your in-app notification feed.

## What appears in the feed

- Workflow runs you triggered manually (success or failure)
- Runs awaiting your approval (manual approval steps)
- Mentions in workflow comments
- Team invitations and role changes
- Billing alerts

## Mark as read / unread

Click any notification to mark it read. Read notifications are dimmed but not removed. Click **Clear all** to remove all read notifications.

## Notification settings

**Settings → Notifications → In-app** lets you choose which event types appear in your feed.

## Real-time updates

The feed updates in real-time without page reload. The bell icon shows a count of unread notifications.

## Audio alerts

Optionally enable audio alerts for high-priority notifications (workflow failures, approvals waiting). Configure under **Settings → Notifications → Audio**.
