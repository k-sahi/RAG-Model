# Teams

Group users into teams for easier permission management.

## Creating teams

**Settings → Teams → New team**. Provide a name and an optional description.

## Adding members

Click a team and use **Add members** to assign users. A user can belong to multiple teams.

## Team-scoped permissions

Use teams to:

- Restrict workflow visibility (only members of Team X can see Team X's workflows)
- Restrict connection usage (only members of Team Y can use Team Y's integrations)
- Send team-wide notifications

## Default behavior

By default, all workflows and connections are workspace-wide visible. Team scoping must be explicitly enabled per resource.

## Team admins

Each team can have one or more team admins. Team admins can manage team membership but do not gain workspace-level admin permissions.
