# Workflow Templates

Helix offers pre-built templates for common use cases. Use them as a starting point or learning resource.

## Browsing templates

Navigate to **Templates** in the sidebar. Templates are organized by category:

- **Sales**: Lead routing, deal stage automation, follow-up reminders
- **Support**: Ticket triage, SLA monitoring, escalation
- **Ops**: New employee onboarding, equipment requests, time-off approvals
- **Marketing**: Campaign syndication, lead nurturing, social posting
- **Finance**: Invoice processing, expense approvals, vendor management

## Installing a template

Click **Install** on any template. You will be prompted to:

1. Choose a workspace (if you belong to multiple)
2. Connect required integrations (or use existing connections)
3. Customize step configurations

The installed workflow appears in your Workflows list, in Inactive state. Review and activate when ready.

## Modifying templates

Installed templates are full workflows — you can edit them like any other. Changes you make do not affect the original template.

## Submitting templates

If you have built a workflow you think would help others, submit it via **Templates → Submit yours**. Approved submissions are credited to your workspace.
