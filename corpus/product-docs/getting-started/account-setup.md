# Account Setup

This guide walks you through creating and configuring your Helix account for the first time.

## 1. Create your account

Visit https://app.helix.io/signup and enter your work email. You will receive a verification email within 2 minutes. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder or contact support@helix.io.

## 2. Verify your email

Click the verification link in the email. The link expires after 24 hours. If your link expires, return to the signup page and request a new one.

## 3. Create your workspace

After verification, you will be prompted to create a workspace. Workspace names must be:

- Between 3 and 32 characters
- Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only
- Unique across Helix

You cannot rename a workspace after creation, so choose carefully.

## 4. Invite teammates

Once your workspace is created, navigate to **Settings → Team** to invite teammates. The number of users you can invite depends on your plan:

- Starter: 1 user
- Pro: up to 10 users
- Enterprise: unlimited users

## 5. Connect your first integration

Head to **Integrations** and connect any app from our supported list. Most integrations use OAuth and take less than a minute.
