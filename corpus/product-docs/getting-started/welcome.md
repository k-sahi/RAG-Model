# Welcome to Helix

Helix is a workflow automation platform that connects your apps, automates repetitive tasks, and helps your team focus on what matters.

## What you can do with Helix

- Connect 50+ business apps including Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Google Drive, and more.
- Build workflows visually with our drag-and-drop builder.
- Schedule workflows with cron expressions.
- Trigger workflows from webhooks, app events, or manual approvals.
- Monitor every workflow run with detailed logs.
