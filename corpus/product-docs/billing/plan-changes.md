# Changing Plans

Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan from **Settings → Billing → Plan**.

## Upgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately. Helix prorates the difference between your old and new plan for the remainder of the current billing period.

After upgrade:
- New plan limits apply right away.
- New features (integrations, API access, etc.) are unlocked.
- Existing data and workflows are preserved.

## Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Until then, you keep your current plan's features.

When the downgrade activates:
- New plan limits apply.
- Features no longer in your plan become unavailable. Workflows that depend on disabled features will fail.
- We do not delete data when you downgrade.

## Cancellation

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain full access until then.

After cancellation:
- Workflows continue running through the end of the period.
- After the period ends, workflows are paused and the workspace is read-only for 90 days.
- After 90 days, the workspace is permanently deleted unless you reactivate.

## Annual to monthly

Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at your next renewal. We do not refund prorated amounts on annual plans.

## Refunds

We do not offer refunds for partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, you continue to have access until the period ends.
