# Invoices and Receipts

Access your billing history and receipts under **Settings → Billing**.

## Viewing invoices

**Settings → Billing → Invoices** lists all past invoices with status (paid, pending, overdue) and amount. Click any invoice to download the PDF.

## Invoice contents

Each invoice includes:

- Invoice number and date
- Billing period
- Plan name and seat count
- Line items (subscription, overage charges, add-ons)
- Subtotal, tax, total
- Payment status

## Tax handling

Helix collects sales tax in jurisdictions where required. Tax is shown as a line item.

For VAT/GST, provide your VAT/GST number under **Settings → Billing → Tax info**. Reverse-charge applies for B2B customers in eligible jurisdictions.

## Custom invoice fields

Enterprise customers can add PO numbers, custom remit-to addresses, or custom line item descriptions. Configure these under **Settings → Billing → Invoice customization**.

## Downloading historical invoices

All invoices are retained indefinitely. Download in bulk via **Settings → Billing → Export all**.
